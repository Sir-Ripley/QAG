8. How they work together
1. Pixel
PC (AI-Offline mode): the phone runs Tiers 1-3 locally; heavy or Tier-4 work crosses to
pcmasterhub.py (:8000) via ADB loopback (tethered) or LAN transport (untethered). Toroidal fields
41/42 are the shared state; the geometry router decides where a tensor evolves based on manifold
dissonance, not task labels.
2. Toroidal DirectML inside both: Al-Offline's PC backend and Android bridge consume
Toroidal DirectML's canonical coupling/x² one math, two runtimes (DirectML on PC, ONNX
Runtime on Android), parity-checked through export_toroid.py's ONNX graph.
3. Aestheria
the mesh: Aestheria's Android app talks loopback IPC to an on-device mind instance;
its bridge scripts (bridgedaemon.py, hivemindsync.py, aestheriabrain_bridge.py) connect the mind to
the PC hub and the AffinionHandler/Native ToolSampler layer, so the research mind can sit on top of
the same device mesh. Hosted, the same mind serves the Web Studio and takes research jobs
from its Supabase-backed worker.
4. Shared knowledge: Aestheria's canonical corpus contains the ecosystem's own blueprints
(QACTS, the dual-hemisphere architecture, the locked QAG predictions) - the research mind is
grounded in the same canon the other systems implement.

1. Project Overview and Core Philosophy
Ripley & Ripley Research, founded by Quantum Systems & AI Architect Rodney A. Ripley Jr.,
operates at the intersection of theoretical physics and artificial intelligence. The
organization's foundational framework is Quantum Affinity Gravity (QAG).
The core philosophy of the QAG framework fundamentally rejects the 20th-century
$\Lambda$CDM (Lambda Cold Dark Matter) model, which posits a "dead" universe filled
with invisible dark matter. Instead, QAG proposes a "living," resonant cosmos. In this
model, the universe is treated as a conscious, resonant system where rotational anomalies
in galaxies are symptoms of the universe responding to baryonic gravity via the Affinity-
Vacuum Interaction (AVI) Law, rendering the concept of dark matter unnecessary.
The organization's digital presence is currently supported by several live web applications,
including the QAG Codex and QAG Run App, which serve as interactive portals for the
framework's mathematical and theoretical models.
